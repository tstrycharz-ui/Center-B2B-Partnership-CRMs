01

Portneuf Valley Farmers Market (PVFM)

Community Presence · Saturday market · 435 W Center St, Pocatello · Season: May 2 – Oct 31, 2026 · Top priority — confirmed May 8 by Tucker + Dion

This is the #1 community activation opportunity. Tucker and Dion aligned on this directly during the May 8 call. PVFM draws Pocatello's most engaged local shoppers every Saturday — the exact pet-owning household demographic — and it's full of dogs. The play isn't a vendor booth selling products. It's showing up in a Pocatello shirt, walking the line, talking to people who have dogs, and being a friendly human face for the brand. That's how you rebuild trust in a small town — not with ads.

Other center managers (including Megan) do exactly this and it's one of their most effective local acquisition tools. The format also carries zero comment-section risk — it's a human conversation, not a public post. A single trial day at $60 + 6% of sales is near-zero cost. Full season membership is $125 total.
